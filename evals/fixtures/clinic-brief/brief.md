# CareSlot: brief

CareSlot is a mobile app (iOS and Android) for patients of small medical clinics. Patients book, move and cancel appointments; clinics get fewer no-shows.

Draft stories:

- **CARE-1 Book an appointment.** A patient picks a clinic, a reason for the visit, a practitioner and a time slot, then confirms.
- **CARE-2 Reschedule or cancel.** A patient moves or cancels an upcoming appointment.
- **CARE-3 Appointment reminders.** The patient gets a reminder the day before, with a way to confirm or cancel.

Context: many patients are over 65. The app launches in English and Spanish in the US. Some clinics charge a no-show fee.
